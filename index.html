Why a bar

All the clean. None of the plastic.

Some shampoo bars are really just soap, and soap can leave hair feeling waxy or dry. Drop is different. It's real shampoo, made into a bar.

  • It works like your shampoo. Rich lather, clean rinse, soft hair.
  • It's kind to your hair. Made without sulfates, silicones, or parabens.
  • It cuts down on plastic. Every bar is one less bottle to throw away.
  • It saves space. One small bar instead of a shelf full of bottles.

We're finishing our recipes with a cosmetic chemist. The full ingredient list will be posted here before launch.

Better for the planet

One small switch. A lot less plastic.

Every bottle of shampoo you finish ends up in the trash or the recycling bin. A Drop bar comes in a paper box, and when the bar is gone, there's nothing left behind.

How to use

Easy as one, two, three, four

Never used a shampoo bar? It takes about ten seconds to learn.

  1. 1

    Wet

    Soak your hair and get the bar wet.

  2. 2

    Lather

    Rub the shampoo bar on your head a few times, then massage with your fingers.

  3. 3

    Rinse and condition

    Rinse it out. Slide the conditioner bar down the ends of your hair, wait a minute, and rinse again.

  4. 4

    Let it dry

    Put the bars back on the tin, away from the water, so they stay firm for next time.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this just a bar of soap?

No. Regular soap can leave hair feeling waxy or dry. Drop bars are made with the same kind of gentle cleansers used in bottled shampoo, pressed into a solid bar.

What makes it eco-friendly?

There's no plastic bottle. The bar comes in a paper box, and it's small and light to ship. The Starter Kit tin can be reused for every bar after that.

How long does a bar last?

Our testers are finding that out right now. We'll share real numbers here before launch.

Will it work on curly or coily hair?

Our first bars are made for normal hair. Tell us your hair type when you sign up, because it helps us decide what to make next.

Is it safe for color-treated hair?

We haven't tested that yet, so we won't promise it. When we have, we'll say so here.

How do I keep the bar from getting mushy?

Keep it out of standing water. The tin in the Starter Kit lets water drain away so the bar dries between showers.
