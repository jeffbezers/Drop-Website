## Direction contract

THESIS: The shower itself is the brand surface. Drop owns "the deep end of haircare without the bottle": a glazed tile wall you descend section by section. It refuses the category default of a cream/kraft eco page with botanical sprigs and a split hero beside a product shot.

OWN-WORLD: Glazed square tile with warm off-white grout as the ground. Aqua at the surface, stepping through seven glaze tones to deep teal at 3.0 m. Pool depth-marker plaques: white tile with deep-teal condensed numerals. Bars are pigmented drops (citrus-yellow shampoo, coral conditioner). Type: Anybody (width axis, condensed for markers, wide-heavy for headlines) plus Hanken Grotesk for body. Controls are tile-edged: square corners, grout-coloured borders, deep-teal fills.

STORY: Bars have failed you before (waxy, drying, mushy). Drop is syndet, not soap. Here's the lineup and the four-step ritual. Would you switch? Join the testers.
